# Framework Standards

Every page within the Assessment Informed Teaching Framework follows the same structure.

---

## 1. Big Idea

Explain the concept in one sentence.

---

## 2. Why it Matters

Show the impact when the concept is present and when it is missing.

---

## 3. Professional Reasoning

Guide educators through the thinking process.

---

## 4. Professional Walkthrough

Use an authentic classroom or leadership example.

---

## 5. Try It Yourself

Provide an opportunity to apply the thinking.

---

## 6. Department Connections

Explain where Department resources support this part of the process.

---

## 7. Reflection

Prompt educators to evaluate their own professional reasoning.

---

## 8. Continue the Journey

Offer connected pathways to the next stage of professional reasoning.

---

## Design Principles

Keep content concise.

Use diagrams wherever possible.

Prioritise authentic examples.

Respect educators as professionals.

Keep students at the centre of every decision.

Develop capability, not compliance.
