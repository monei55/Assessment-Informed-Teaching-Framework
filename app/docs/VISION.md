# Assessment Informed Teaching Framework

## A Professional Reasoning Model for Developing Educator Capability

---

## Purpose

To develop the professional capability of educators by making expert professional reasoning visible, practical and transferable.

The framework supports teachers, instructional coaches and school leaders to connect curriculum, assessment, pedagogy and evidence into one coherent professional reasoning process that improves instructional decision-making and student outcomes.

---

## Vision

We believe every student deserves teaching that is intentional, evidence-informed and responsive.

This begins with educators who understand not only what to teach, but why, how and how they will know students are learning.

---

## Guiding Principle

Every feature within this framework should strengthen professional reasoning.

If it does not strengthen professional capability, it does not belong.

---

Professional Reasoning

↓

Professional Capability

↓

Instructional Decision Making

↓

Student Success
